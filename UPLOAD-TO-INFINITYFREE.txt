INFINITYFREE UPLOAD
==================

Upload these files from the ZIP root (NOT from a subfolder):

  index.html
  index.php
  admin.html
  api/
  assets/
  css/
  js/
  admin/
  uploads/
  vehicles/
  ...

Into InfinityFree htdocs so you have:

  htdocs/index.html
  htdocs/index.php

Do not create an extra "htdocs" folder inside the ZIP — site files are already at the root.
