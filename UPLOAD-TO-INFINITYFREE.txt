INFINITYFREE UPLOAD
==================

1. Open the folder named:  htdocs
2. Upload EVERYTHING inside "htdocs" into your InfinityFree "htdocs" directory.
3. After upload you must have:

   YOUR_INFINITYFREE_htdocs/
     index.html
     index.php
     admin.html
     api/
     assets/
     css/
     js/
     ...

Do NOT upload the outer "Tesla-main" folder.
Do NOT upload only the "docs" folder name — use the "htdocs" folder contents.
